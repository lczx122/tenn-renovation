Our story

We make what we sell.

我们亲手打造所售的一切。

Tenn Renovation began in 2001 with a small cabinet workshop and a simple promise: template it properly, build it properly, and stand behind it. Word spread the way it does in Melaka — one kitchen, one wardrobe, one neighbour at a time.

As homeowners asked us to handle more of the home, we added painting crews, a steel fabrication and powder-coating line, and a smart-home team — each one built in-house rather than subcontracted, so the standard never changed hands.

Today Tenn fits out whole homes and developer showrooms, and the people who answer the phone are still the people who built the work.

What never changed

Three promises, kept since 2001.
