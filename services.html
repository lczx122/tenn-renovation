Iron work, in-house

From raw steel to your front door.

从钢材到家门，铁艺全程自家打造。

Every gate, grille and railing is cut, welded, powder-coated and fitted by our own team. Here's the build, step by step — tap any photo or play a clip.

01 · Fabrication

Cut, drilled & welded to size

Raw steel is measured, cut and welded into frame on the workshop floor — the bones of every gate and grille.

02 · Powder coating

An even, weather-proof finish

Finished frames are powder-coated in our spray booth and cured — a hard, consistent coat that stands up to sun and rain for years.

03 · Install & handover

Fitted on site, checked with you

We fit each piece square and secure, seal the gaps, and walk you through the finished work before we leave.
