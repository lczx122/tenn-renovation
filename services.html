What we do

Four crafts, finished to one standard.

We're one team that designs, makes and installs across the whole home — so your cabinetry, finishes, systems and metalwork all line up the way they should.

01 — Our signature craft

Customized Cabinets

Kitchens, wardrobes, TV consoles, vanities and storage — designed around your space and built in our own workshop. We template on site so everything sits flush, with no filler strips or awkward gaps.

  • Full kitchen design: islands, tall units, appliance integration
  • Fitted wardrobes & walk-ins with internal organisation
  • Moisture-resistant carcasses, edged on all six faces
  • Soft-close hinges & runners as standard
  • Laminate, veneer, 2-pac spray or solid timber finishes
  • Integrated mirror & glass work — wardrobe doors, vanities & display panels

02

Smart Home Systems

Lighting, climate, security and entertainment, set up to work together and stay genuinely simple to use. We plan the wiring during renovation so nothing is bolted on as an afterthought.

  • Smart lighting & scene control, room by room
  • Door entry, CCTV & alarm integration
  • Automated curtains, climate & ventilation
  • Voice & app control, with simple manual overrides
  • Tidy, future-ready cabling planned into the build

03

Painting Work

Interior and exterior painting done the proper way — it's the prep that makes a finish last. We protect, fill, sand and prime before a brush touches the wall.

  • Interior repaints, feature walls & ceilings
  • Exterior & facade painting with weather-grade coatings
  • Crack filling, skim & dustless sanding
  • Sharp cut-in lines and even, consistent coats
  • Low-VOC options for family & nursery spaces

04

Iron Grills & Gates

Hand-fabricated grills, gates and railings that secure the home without making it feel like a cage. We design the metalwork to match your facade, then weather-treat it to last.

  • Main gates: swing, sliding & modern slat designs
  • Window & balcony grills in custom patterns
  • Staircase & balcony railings
  • Hot-dip / powder-coat finishes for rust resistance
  • Optional smart-lock & auto-gate integration
