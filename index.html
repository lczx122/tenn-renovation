The cabinetry difference

Measured by hand. Made to fit.

Every kitchen starts with a site visit, not a catalogue. We template your walls, account for every pipe and skirting, and build cabinetry that sits flush — no filler strips, no gaps, no compromises.

  • CarcassMoisture-resistant board, edged on all six faces
  • HardwareSoft-close hinges & runners, 25-year rated
  • FinishesLaminate, veneer, 2-pac spray or solid timber

How we work

01

02

Our story

A Melaka workshop, 25 years in the making.

Tenn Renovation started as a small cabinet workshop and grew, project by project, into a full-home renovation team. Three things never changed: we make what we sell, we finish what we start, and we stand behind it long after the dust settles.

Today we serve homeowners across Melaka — with plans to bring the same standard further afield.

— Royce & Lucas, Tenn Renovation
