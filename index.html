Common questions

Renovating in Melaka? Start here.

马六甲装修常见问题。

Straight answers to the questions homeowners ask us first. Anything else, message Royce on WhatsApp.

Do you handle the whole renovation, or only cabinets?

The whole house. Most of our work is full-home renovation: interior design and 3D plans, custom cabinets, painting, iron grills and gates, electrical and aluminium work, plus tiling, plaster ceilings, partitions, aircon installation and leak repair. You deal with one contact from design to handover.

Which areas do you serve?

We are based in Melaka and serve the whole state and surrounding areas, including Ayer Keroh, Bukit Beruang, Bukit Baru, Klebang, Kota Syahbandar, Pulau Gadong, Melaka Raya, Batu Berendam, Alor Gajah and Jasin.

Is the site visit and quote free?

Yes. We visit, measure and template the space, then send a clear itemised quote with no obligation. Message Royce on WhatsApp or use the quote form to arrange it.

Do you provide interior design and 3D drawings?

Yes. For whole-house and package renovations we prepare 3D models and plans first, so you can see the layout, cabinets and finishes before any work starts.

Can you do a fully furnished package for a new home?

Yes. We deliver fully furnished packages for new homes, including the cabinet packages fitted for 300+ homes at Ambience Residence, Pulau Gadong, and the fully furnished package planned for Ambience Kota Syahbandar.

How does a project run once we agree?

Every trade follows a written eight-step SOP: confirm the order, measure on site, customise, set the installation date, install, quality checks, clean up and handover. You know what happens on site and what comes next, and our after-sales staff follow up afterwards.
