Who we are

One team. Four crafts. The whole home.

一个团队，四项工艺，整个家。

Tenn Renovation started in 2001 as a small cabinet workshop in Melaka. Project by project it grew into a full-home renovation team that still makes what it sells: cabinets milled in our own workshop, gates and grilles welded and powder-coated in our own booth, painting and smart-home systems fitted by our own crews.

Because nothing is subcontracted, the details line up — the kitchen matches the wardrobes, the gate matches the facade, and one team answers the phone long after handover.

Why Tenn

Built in-house. Documented. Backed for years.

自家打造，流程规范，长期保障。

Three things set a Tenn renovation apart — and they are the same three things investors and developer partners ask us about.

01

Made in our own workshop

Cabinets are milled and edged in-house; steel is cut, welded and powder-coated in our own booth. No subcontractor mark-ups, no waiting on someone else's schedule.

02

A written SOP for every job

Each craft follows a documented eight-step standard from order confirmation to handover, so you always know what happens on site and what comes next.

03

After-sales you can call

Dedicated staff follow up after installation. The same team that built it is the team that picks up the phone — and 300+ Ambience Pulau Gadong homeowners can vouch for that.
